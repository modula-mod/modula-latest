# Phase 7M-B6 — Vault Notes artwork

Three refined systems were considered. One shipped.

## Concepts

1. **Folded private page** — nested sheet, dog-ear fold, quiet stitch. Chosen.
2. **Sealed knowledge card** — stacked cards with a closed seam. Strong, but read as a wallet.
3. **Archive ribbon** — vertical ribbon through a page. Too decorative at 44pt.

## Chosen system

Folded private page.

- Symbol: folded sheet with three ink lines and a scarce stitch
- Icon: works at 44 / 56 / 64
- Accent: ivory / iron ink / warm umber (`#F4EDE1`, `#2A241C`, `#8A6A4B`)
- Hero: same mark, larger wash
- Fallback: generic cube only when a pack is missing

Avoided: padlock, vault door, teal cube, generic document-only icon.

## Other first-party packs

Tasks, CRM, GAIA Atlas, Avatar Studio, Gubic remain concept packs. They are not invented catalogue products.
