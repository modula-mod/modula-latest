# Phase 1 Social UI Inspiration

Phase 1 studies successful social-product patterns without cloning another application. The implementation must remain recognisably Modula: profile-first, modular, governed, and calm.

## Instagram-Inspired Patterns

- Study restrained feed chrome, story/avatar rails, profile media grids, clear content-first hierarchy, familiar bottom navigation, and compact social actions.
- Do not copy Instagram branding, proprietary icons, gradients, exact spacing, or exact layout measurements.

## Facebook-Inspired Patterns

- Study richer post metadata, community visibility, feed diversity, quick interaction controls, meaningful comment previews, and secondary navigation through a menu.
- Avoid excessively dense card stacks, crowded action rows, and too many competing shortcuts.

## TikTok-Inspired Patterns

- Study immersive full-height media, vertically paged content, strong creator attribution, right-side action rails, minimal media chrome, and immediate interaction.
- Do not turn the primary Modula feed into a video-only experience.

## Bluesky-Inspired Patterns

- Study text readability, feed selector clarity, compact post information, thread hierarchy, account and feed flexibility, and protocol separation concepts.
- Do not copy Bluesky's provider tree, session architecture, route system, AT Protocol calls, or global gesture infrastructure.

## Modula Identity

- Board access must be visible.
- Module cards and module-aware posts should feel installed into the environment.
- Composer Tools are a first hint of a future tool system.
- Profile tabs must be ready to host modules and widgets.
- Use the Modula teal accent through semantic tokens only.
- Custom feed types and user-controlled environments are core to the shell.

The target is a Modula social operating shell informed by proven product principles, not several products visually merged together.
